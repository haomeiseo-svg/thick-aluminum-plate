PVDF color precoated aluminum coils

PVDF coating has excellent durability in various climates, excellent resistance to UV - radiation and high resistance to corrosion. This coating can withstand 20 years in the open air, retaining its color and gloss in its original form. Thanks to the excellent stability of the goods to the direct mechanical effects and ductility, it is easy to handle. Colored tape is coated and embossed to be processed. Aluminium tape coated with PVDF - ideal decorative material for the production of facial sheet of aluminum composite panels, which are widely used in finishing the vertical facades of the aerodrome, exhibition and administrative centers, hotels, etc.
