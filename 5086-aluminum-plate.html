5086 aluminum plate

This 5086 aluminum plate has higher strength than 5052 aluminum plate and 5083 aluminum plate. 5086 aluminum plate is particularly resistant to salt water corrosion. .but it is not strengthened by heat treatment, instead becoming stronger due to strain hardening or cold mechanical working of the material.3.0%-4.5% of magnesium is added to5086 aluminum plate , which enable it to have high strength. Heat treatment does not strongly affect the strength, so 5086 aluminum plate can be readily welded and retain most of its mechanical strength. 5086 aluminum plate mainly has four tempers that are vastly used, including 5086-H, 5086-H32, 5086-H112 as well as 5086-H116. 5086 aluminum plate is the preferred hull material for small aluminium boats or larger yachts. Its high strength and good corrosion resistance make it an excellent match for yachting. 5086 aluminum plate has a tendency to undergo Stress corrosion cracking and is not used much in aircraft construction as a result. 5086 aluminum plate has been used in vehicle armor, notably in the M113 Armored Personnel Carrier and M2 Bradley Infantry fighting vehicle.
