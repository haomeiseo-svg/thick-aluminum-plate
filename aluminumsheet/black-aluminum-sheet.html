black aluminum sheet

Our black anodized aluminum sheet finish is very resistant to abrasion and is perfectly suitable for brake forming.
The anodized aluminum sheets are recommended for outdoor use because of their fade resistance and can be used in a wide variety of unique design application.
Our black anodized aluminum sheets are available in gauges of.032, .040, .063. haomei aluminium provides a large selection of stock sizes but also welcome custom size inquires. All sheets are leveled, sheeted, blanked and slit.
