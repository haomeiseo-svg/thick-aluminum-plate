6082 aluminum sheet

6082 aluminum sheet is a medium strength alloy sheet with excellent corrosion resistance. Alloy 6082 has the highest strength of the 6000 series aluminum alloy sheet. Due to the higher strength of Alloy 6082 it has replaced Alloy 6061 in many applications. Alloy 6082 is typically used in highly stressed applications, Trusses, Bridges, Cranes, Transport applications, Ore Skips, Beer Barrels, Milk churns

6082 aluminum sheet Key Features:

6082 aluminum sheet offers good machinability with excellent corrosion resistance. Available in bar, plate, sheet and tube, the alloy is used in structural applications and is preferred to 6061 aluminum sheet.
