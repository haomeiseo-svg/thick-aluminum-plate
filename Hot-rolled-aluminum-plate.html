Hot rolled aluminum plate

Hot rolled aluminum plate refers to those whose thickness is more than 4.5mm and widely used in industrial applications, tank car aluminum cans with aluminum plate, grinding tool with aluminum, a container with aluminum plate, pressure vessel, automotive, construction machinery with aluminum plate, etc.

Hot rolled aluminum plate has an added uniform annealing process, such as composition segregation, such as impurity defects are relatively easy to solve. The quality of the products are higher than that of cast-rolling aluminum plate, hot rolled aluminum plate on deep drawing performance, bending performance is far higher than that of cast-rolling aluminum plate, the price also is higher than cast-rolling aluminum plate.

Common aluminum plate of hot rolled includes 1series, 1100, 1060, 1070, 1050, 3 series 3003, 3004, 3005, 3104, 3005, 5 series of 5082, 5182, 5083, 5754, 5083, 6, 6061, 6082, 6083, etc.
